# RP AgentOS Blueprint

**Version:** 1.0\
**Owner:** Retailpulses GK

## Vision

RP AgentOS is an AI-native Commerce Operating System that enables a very
small team to operate ecommerce businesses with the productivity,
consistency, and scalability of a much larger organization.

The system is designed around **business capabilities**, not individual
AI agents.

## Mission

Enable two people to operate ecommerce businesses that traditionally
require teams of ten or more by combining structured business workflows,
AI automation, and human supervision.

## Strategic Objectives

-   Automate approximately **80%** of repetitive ecommerce operations.
-   Expand operational depth beyond human capacity through AI-first
    workflows.
-   Build reusable business capabilities instead of isolated automation
    scripts.
-   Create a platform designed for future commercialization.

## Core Technology Stack

-   **Database:** Supabase (single source of truth)
-   **Cloud:** Cloudflare
-   **Source Control:** GitHub
-   **Development:** VS Code + AI coding agents
-   **Primary AI:** Local Qwen models via Ollama
-   **Strategic AI:** OpenAI reasoning models
-   **External Systems:** GigaB2B, Marketplace APIs

## AI Routing

**Local AI (default)** - Operational tasks - Classification - Draft
generation - Batch processing

**OpenAI** - Architecture - Strategic planning - Complex reasoning -
Creative work - Difficult edge cases

## Human-by-Exception

Humans intervene only when: - confidence is low - financial risk is
high - policy approval is required - creative judgment is needed
