# RP AgentOS Principles

These principles are the long-term constitution of RP AgentOS.

## 1. Business Before Technology

Every feature must solve a real business problem before discussing
implementation.

## 2. Capability Before Agent

Business capabilities are permanent assets.

Agents are replaceable execution interfaces.

## 3. AI First

Assume AI executes every workflow by default.

Humans supervise exceptions.

## 4. Human by Exception

Human work should focus on approval, creativity, negotiation, and
strategic decisions.

## 5. Data is the Source of Truth

Supabase is the master business database.

Business knowledge belongs in structured data, never only inside
prompts.

## 6. Workflow Before UI

Design workflows first.

User interfaces come later.

## 7. Local AI by Default

Use local Qwen models whenever quality is sufficient.

Cloud AI should be reserved for reasoning-intensive or high-value tasks.

## 8. Modular Architecture

Capabilities must be independently deployable and reusable.

Marketplace-specific logic belongs in adapters.

## 9. Measurable Automation

Every workflow must define: - success metric - automation rate - human
intervention rate - business value

## 10. Commercialization Ready

Every capability should be evaluated for: - reuse - configuration -
multi-tenancy - SaaS potential

## 11. Simplicity Wins

Prefer the simplest architecture that can scale for the next three
years.

Avoid unnecessary infrastructure and over-engineering.

## 12. Continuous Challenge

Architectural decisions should be questioned regularly.

Optimize for maintainability, business value, and long-term scalability
rather than short-term convenience.
